About the ticket

Experienced leaders with roots in the territory.

Oakland Benta is a former senator and former St. Croix Police Chief, while Merwin Potter is a former Fire Service Director. Together, they bring public-safety leadership, service-oriented governance, and practical knowledge of the challenges facing families and communities across the U.S. Virgin Islands.

The campaign’s promise is straightforward: invest in people, restore trust, and create opportunity while making the territory safer and more resilient.
