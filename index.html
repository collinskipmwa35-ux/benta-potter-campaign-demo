About the ticket

Experienced leaders with roots in the territory.

Oakland Benta is a former senator and former St. Croix Police Chief, while Merwin Potter is a former Fire Service Director. Together, they represent public safety leadership, service-oriented governance, and a practical understanding of the everyday challenges facing families and communities across the U.S. Virgin Islands.

Their campaign centers on a simple but powerful promise: build a more resilient territory by investing in people, restoring trust, and creating opportunities that reflect the strength of the Virgin Islands community.

Campaign manifesto

A vision for a stronger Virgin Islands.

1

Restore confidence in leadership

Put the people first by strengthening accountability, making transparent decisions, and rebuilding trust in Government operations.

2

Protect the well-being of families

Expand access to healthcare, support essential workers, and ensure the territory has the emergency and medical capacity needed for long-term care.

3

Grow opportunity at home

Attract investment, increase industrial and entrepreneurial activity, and create pathways for local workers and business owners to thrive.

4

Build safer communities

Strengthen collaboration between agencies, deliver practical public safety solutions, and ensure residents feel secure in their neighborhoods.

5

Lead with service

Root every policy decision in the lived realities of residents and the long-term betterment of the territory.
